Community rules

Let’s keep Havra welcoming for everyone sharing a little of life across Southeast Asia.

  • Be kind and respectful

    Disagree kindly. No bullying, threats, hate speech, or harassment.

  • Share appropriate content

    No explicit sexual content, graphic violence, spam, or scams.

  • Protect people’s privacy

    Ask before sharing photos or videos of others. Don’t post private details.

  • Report harmful content

    Use Report when you see harmful content or behavior. Don’t repost it.

  • Block unwanted contact

    Use Block if you don’t want contact from an account.
