Guidelines

COMMUNITY RULES

Be kind, be real

Your voice, your rules — 7 seconds at a time

NOT ALLOWED

  • Sexual or explicit content
  • Harassment, hate or threats
  • Sharing private contact details
  • Scams, ads or impersonation
  • Clips that aren’t your own voice

Report or block

Report a clip or a user if they break these rules. Block a user to stop further contact.

Rule violations may lead to removal of clips or accounts.
