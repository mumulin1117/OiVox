# OiVox discovery media v3

The bundled discovery voices originate from the `google/fleurs` dataset and the CSTR VCTK Corpus mirror `sanchit-gandhi/vctk`, as recorded in the user-provided v3 source inventory.

Avatars and voices are independently sourced test fixtures. A pairing exists only for local product demonstration and does not claim that the person pictured is the speaker. These fixtures must not be presented as live users or live messages.

See `docs/data/discovery-media-v3.md` and `src/data/discoveryMediaManifest.js` for the stable pair IDs and source-row correspondence. Dataset licensing and release requirements must be reviewed before distribution outside the local review build.
